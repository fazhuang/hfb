# HFB Research Platform

HFB is a digital humanities research platform centered on Huangfu Mi and the
_Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing_. Its domain language prioritizes verifiable research
objects and evidence-backed scholarly work.

## Language

**Huangfu Mi Studies Platform**:
A professional institutional research environment dedicated to deepening,
broadening, and coordinating scholarship about Huangfu Mi.
_Avoid_: content website, digital museum, general AI assistant

**AI-assisted Scholarly Research**:
Human-led research in which AI helps discover, compare, analyze, and organize
evidence while researchers remain responsible for scholarly conclusions.
_Avoid_: autonomous research, AI-generated scholarship

**Evidence-backed Version Comparison Workflow**:
The first complete research workflow: find a passage, compare it across two
editions, inspect its sources and evidence, record a research note, and export
a research record with citations.
_Avoid_: MVP demo, version page, AI comparison

**Research Record**:
An exportable scholarly output that preserves the compared passages, edition
identities, evidence, citations, and the researcher's notes.
_Avoid_: AI answer, chat transcript, report

**Research Note**:
A researcher-authored interpretation or observation attached to the evidence
examined during a research workflow.
_Avoid_: AI response, comment

**Evidence**:
A verifiable source object supporting a displayed text, relationship, or
claim, with enough provenance for independent checking.
_Avoid_: context, reference data

**Citation**:
The stable, human-readable pointer from a research claim or record to its
supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: link, source label

**Pilot Research Group**:
The invitation-only group of 3-5 people that validates the first research
workflow, including subject and versionology expertise and the Product Owner.
_Avoid_: public users, beta users, test accounts

**Partner Research Institution**:
The organization for which HFB is deployed and which supplies or authorizes the
research corpus used by its Pilot Research Group.
_Avoid_: customer, public, content owner
